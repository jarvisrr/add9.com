Frequently asked questions

What is the classic J-pop chord progression?

The Royal Road progression (IVmaj7 – V7 – iii7 – vi7) is a staple of J-pop and anime music, often colored with add9, sus4, and slash-bass inversions.

How do J-pop guitarists voice rock chords cleanly?

By holding a common fifth or ninth on the top strings while moving the bass note underneath.
