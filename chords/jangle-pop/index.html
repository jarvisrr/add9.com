Frequently asked questions

Why do jangle pop players use Whole Step Up or Open E?

Tuning a whole step up (or using a capo on fret 2) increases string tension and brightness, giving open-string arpeggios their signature chime.

Are Standard tuning jangle pop chords included?

Yes—this guide covers Standard tuning alongside Open E and Whole Step Up shapes.
