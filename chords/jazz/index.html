Playable Jazz voice-leading study (Standard tuning)

Demonstrates how Jazz players move smoothly from a 3-note Cm7 shell and tense C7b9 into warm Cmaj9 and C6/9 resolutions with minimal left-hand jump.

  • Mute the A string on low-E shells: On Cm7 (8x88xx) and C7b9 (8x8989), let your index finger rest gently against the A string to mute it.
  • Notice the 1-finger move from Cmaj9 to C6/9: Compare x3243x (Cmaj9) and x3223x (C6/9)—only the G string drops from fret 4 to fret 2.
  • Comp with rhythm, not volume: Three- and four-note shells leave space for the bass and piano while keeping guide tones (3rd and 7th) crystal clear.

Frequently asked questions

What are jazz shell voicings on guitar?

Shell voicings use just three notes—root, 3rd, and 7th—dropping the 5th so you can comp cleanly through ii–V–I changes without clutter.

When should I use Drop 2 voicings?

Drop 2 voicings put four close-harmony notes on adjacent strings, ideal for chord-melody arrangements and smooth voice leading.
