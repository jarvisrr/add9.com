Frequently asked questions

Can you play math rock chords in Standard tuning?

Yes. While FACGCE is famous for ringing open extensions, compact maj7, add9, and 6/9 shapes in Standard tuning deliver the same bright intervals without retuning.

Why is FACGCE popular for math rock?

Tuning to F-A-C-G-C-E turns the open strings into an Fmaj9 chord, letting simple two-finger grips ring against bright upper drones.
