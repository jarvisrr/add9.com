Frequently asked questions

What makes a guitar chord sound like shoegaze?

Ringing open high strings (B and high E) against movable power-chord or maj7 roots, plus wide sus2 and add9 voicings that stay clear under reverb and fuzz.

Do I need alternate tunings for shoegaze chords?

No. All 24 voicings on this page work in Standard tuning (EADGBE) by combining fretted bass shapes with open treble strings.
