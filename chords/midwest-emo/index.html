Frequently asked questions

What tunings are best for Midwest emo chords?

FACGCE is the signature open tuning for sparkling arpeggios, while Standard tuning with a capo on frets 2–5 works well for open-string maj7 and add9 shapes.

What chord types give the classic Midwest emo sound?

Major 7th, add9, and sus2 chords with ringing open strings on top create that bittersweet, open harmonic texture.
