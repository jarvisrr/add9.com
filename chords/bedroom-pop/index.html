Frequently asked questions

What chords are most common in bedroom pop?

Relaxed maj7, m7, and add9 shapes in Standard tuning, plus lush Open D (DADF#AD) voicings where one or two fingers create full, shimmering chords.

Can beginners play these bedroom pop voicings?

Yes—many Open D and open-position seventh shapes require only two or three fingers.
