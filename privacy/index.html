add9 Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

add9 is an offline tool for exploring guitar chords and creating chord progressions. Your saved music stays in the app’s storage on your device; add9 does not send it to us.

This policy applies to add9: No more boring chords, developed by Guangxing Ren (“we,” “us,” or “our”). It explains the app’s data practices and what happens if you contact us.

Information in the app

add9 does not require an account. It has no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. The app does not automatically send your personal information, usage activity, or saved content to us or to an app-operated server.

To make its features work, add9 stores the following locally:

  • Your saved chords and their fingerings.
  • Your chord progressions, including names, chord sequences, tempo, capo settings, and creation or modification dates.
  • Preferences such as language, appearance, selected styles, and your most recent chord search, along with onboarding and feature-tip state.

Chord audio is generated and played on your device. add9 does not record audio or request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, or location.

Storage and your choices

add9 does not provide its own cloud synchronization. Your device’s backup services may include app data, depending on your Apple and device settings. Those backups are managed by you and Apple, not by us.

You can delete saved chords and progressions in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device; offloading it may preserve data. Existing backups are managed separately through your device or backup service. We cannot access or delete the music stored on your device.

If you contact us

The feedback option opens your email app. It does not automatically attach your saved chords, progressions, or diagnostic files. If you choose to send a message, we receive your email address, its contents, and any attachments you include.

We use that information to answer your request, resolve issues, and handle privacy questions. Correspondence is processed through our email provider and retained only as long as needed for these purposes or applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete correspondence using the contact address below. We do not sell your personal information or use support emails for advertising.

Apple and TestFlight

Apple handles App Store and device diagnostics under its own privacy practices and your settings. If you use a beta version through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with us; it also shares feedback you submit. Depending on how you joined the test, this may include your name or email address. These are separate from data sent by add9 itself. We use this information to identify and fix app problems, retain any copies only as long as needed for that purpose or legal obligations, and do not share TestFlight information with third parties.

For details, see Apple’s privacy policy and TestFlight and privacy.

This privacy website

Opening this page sends a normal web request to our hosting provider, which may process technical connection information such as your IP address to serve and protect the website. We have not added advertising, analytics scripts, forms, or cookies to this page. This website does not receive your saved add9 music.

Changes to this policy

If add9’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will also be communicated in the app or its release information as appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions, or to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information available to us, contact jarvisrenapps@outlook.com.
